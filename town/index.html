The one-paragraph version

Groups fail from bad sequencing and bad feelings, rarely from lack of gear. Secure drinking water before anything else, put calories in storage before they're needed, beat winter instead of meeting it, write down who does what, and settle disagreements while they're small. Everything else in this section is detail on those five sentences.
