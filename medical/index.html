Educational reference — not a doctor

These pages teach first aid fundamentals for times when professional care is delayed or out of reach. They are no substitute for training, a real clinician, or emergency services. When help is available, use it.

Care for the people around you with what you have on hand. Start with a stocked kit and the two skills that save the most lives before help arrives: stopping severe bleeding and cooling burns correctly.
