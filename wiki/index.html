Four ways to reach deep knowledge

1 · This kit's reader — full .zim packs open in a dedicated reading view with this site's theme, adjustable text size, working links, and shareable article URLs. Pick a pack below.

2 · Full-power server reader — every pack also lives at wiki.w4sh.net with real search, tabs, and deep-linkable URLs. Best for giant libraries like full Wikipedia.

3 · Phones & tablets — mobile browsers here can't hold big libraries well; the free Kiwix Reader app remembers your library between opens.

4 · Authored site pages — the emergency-core articles we rewrote ourselves live under Medical, How-To, Food & Water, and the rest of the tiles; they integrate with global search.

Note: contents inside ZIM packs are searched separately from the global search bar above.
