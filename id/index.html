Identification is not permission

These pages help you recognize plants, wildlife and hazards — they never greenlight eating forage. Never eat any plant, mushroom or berry based on a website entry alone; a wrong guess can kill you. US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. When identification is uncertain at all, the answer is always the same: leave it alone.

Entries are text-first and photo-ready. Each one carries an optional img field pointing at media/id/…; when a photo file exists it renders above the entry, and when it is missing or fails to load you get the letter monogram instead — nothing ever shows as a broken image.
