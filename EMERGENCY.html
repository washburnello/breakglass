EMERGENCY MODE

If a life is in danger right now, call emergency services first, whenever it is possible to do so. This page is for guidance when help is delayed or unavailable. Stay calm, work step by step, and do the next clear thing.

Quick actions

Severe bleedingStop the blood with firm, direct pressure. CPRPush hard and fast in the center of the chest. ChokingBack blows and abdominal thrusts to clear the airway. BurnsCool the burn with clean running water. ShockLie the person down, keep them warm, monitor breathing.
