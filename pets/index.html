Pets & Animals

They count on you twice over

Animals cannot store their own supplies or read an evacuation notice. Everything they need in an outage must already be on your shelf — and the single most common disaster mistake with pets is assuming "we'll figure it out when it happens."

Pet Emergency PrepKits, IDs, carriers, and the evacuate-together rule. Daily Care BasicsFood, water, warmth, and watching for trouble.

Quick numbers

  • 72 hours minimum of pet food and water stored alongside the human supply — a week is better.
  • If you evacuate, they evacuate. Leaving animals behind, even "just for a night," is how pets are lost.
  • Identification beats luck: collar tag + microchip + a current photo on your phone.
  • Livestock and poultry guides ship in a later pass; the principles below scale up more than you'd expect.

Care guidance here is general; your veterinarian knows your animal. Grounded in public-domain FEMA/ready.gov pet preparedness guidance.
