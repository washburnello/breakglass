Bleach disinfection

Drops of plain unscented liquid bleach per batch of water — same numbers as Water Treatment. Stir, wait 30 minutes, sniff for a faint chlorine smell.

Waiting for numbers…

Formula: drops = gallons × 8 (at 6%) or × 6 (at 8.25%), doubled when cloudy · 1 teaspoon ≈ 100 drops.

Bleach has limits

Plain sodium hypochlorite 5–9%, unscented, not expired. Splash-less, thickened, scented, or "color-safe" formulas are not for water treatment, and bleach weakens with age.

Dose helper

Educational only — verify before every dose

This helper mirrors the standard amounts printed on US over-the-counter packaging, for reference when original packaging is lost. It is not medical advice. Verify every dose against the actual product label or a pharmacist/doctor — formulations vary by brand and country. Full tables and rules: Dosing by Weight.

Waiting for numbers…

Method: match weight to the packaging weight bands (identical to Dosing by Weight) — under band minimum means ask a professional, never guess.

Battery bank runtime

Enter capacity directly in watt-hours, or let the amp-hour helper compute it. Efficiency covers inverter losses.

Pack: enter Wh, or Ah with voltage.

Waiting for numbers…

Formula: usable Wh = capacity Wh (or Ah × V) × efficiency ÷ 100 · hours = usable Wh ÷ device watts.

Honesty about cycle life

Lead-acid batteries last far longer if you never drain them past half. Unless the manufacturer says otherwise, plan runtime on 50% of rated capacity — treat a "100 Ah" lead-acid as 50 Ah of real storage. Lithium chemistry tolerates deeper discharge, at higher cost.
