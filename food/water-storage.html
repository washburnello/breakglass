Water Storage

How much

One gallon (about 4 liters) per person per day — roughly half for drinking, half for food prep and hygiene. Three days is the minimum; two weeks is the real target. A family of four aiming for two weeks stores about 56 gallons. Don't forget pets: they drink about an ounce per pound of body weight daily.

At a glance

  • 1 gallon per person per day — half drinking, half food prep and hygiene.
  • 3 days is the minimum; two weeks is the target — about 56 gallons for a family of four.
  • Date every container and rotate the stock once a year.
  • Pets drink about 1 ounce per pound of body weight daily.

Containers, ranked

  1. Factory-sealed bottled water — zero work, stores until the printed date, stack flat packs in a closet.
  2. Food-grade jugs and drums (HDPE, recycling codes 2 or 4): rinse well, fill with tap water treated per Water Treatment, date them, refresh yearly.
  3. Glass jars — fine but heavy, breakable, light-exposed; wrap and darken.

Container rules

Never reuse containers that held milk, juice, or anything sugary — residue breeds bacteria no matter how well you scrub. Never use anything that held fuel, chemicals, or solvents, ever. Milk jugs also biodegrade into leaks within months.

Sanitizing a reusable container

  1. Wash with dish soap and hot water; rinse fully.
  2. Rinse again with a solution of 1 teaspoon unscented bleach per quart of water.
  3. Coat all inside surfaces, dump, air dry upside down, then fill and cap.

Where and how

  • Cool (50–70 °F ideal), dark, away from sunlight and fuels/chemicals — plastic breathes vapor over time.
  • Off concrete floors on wood pallets or shelves; keep out of direct sun.
  • Split storage between locations — kitchen plus basement plus closet beats one big cache in one flood-prone corner.
  • Leave headroom if freezing is possible: 3–4 inches in a rigid jug, laid sideways so ice can split the gap not the jug.

Rotation

Date every container. Once a year (pick a memorable day), rotate: use the old stock for garden/laundry/pets and refill fresh. Treated municipal tap water stores a year easily; untreated well water should be treated before storing.
