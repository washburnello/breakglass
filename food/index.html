Food & Water

The order that matters

Water first — three days without it harms you faster than three weeks without food. Store water, then build a food reserve from meals you already eat, then learn to keep both safe when the power is out.

Quick numbers worth memorizing

  • 1 gallon (4 L) per person per day for drinking and basic hygiene — half drinking, half food/washing.
  • 3 days minimum, 2 weeks better as the storage target for both water and food.
  • 4 hours: how long a closed fridge keeps food safe once power dies.
  • 40 °F (4 °C): the line perishable food must stay below.
  • Treat any unknown water before use — see Water Treatment.
